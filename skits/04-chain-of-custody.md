# Skit 4 — "Chain of Custody"

**Runtime** ~30s · **Format** 9:16 vertical · **Cast** 2

The joke: tracing the document backwards, one honest answer at a time, until the
entire evidentiary foundation turns out to be a guy called Dave.

No real person is named or depicted. The characters are invented.

## Script (clean read)

> **KIT:** Okay. Chain of custody. Where'd you get it?
> **BEX:** Dave.
> **KIT:** Where'd *Dave* get it?
> **BEX:** A group chat.
> **KIT:** *Whose* group chat?
> **BEX:** Dave's.
> **KIT:** ...Who else is in it?
> **BEX:** I don't know. It's Dave's.
> **KIT:** So the chain of custody is Dave.
> **BEX:** The chain of custody is Dave.

---

## Direction notes

- **Play shots 1–6 fast.** Snap the answers in with no air at all — it should
  feel like a rally. The rhythm breaking at shot 7 is what makes shot 7 land.
- BEX is never embarrassed. Every answer is, to BEX, completely satisfactory.
  The moment BEX looks sheepish, the audience stops laughing and starts pitying,
  and the bit dies.
- Shot 11 is agreement, not concession. BEX is confirming a fact they consider
  unremarkable.
- If you have a third person, put them in the background reacting to nothing.

## Alternate ending (swap shot 12)

> **KIT:** Can we talk to Dave?
> **BEX:** *(beat)* Dave left the group chat.

## Alternate ending — darker

> **KIT:** So the chain of custody is Dave.
> **BEX:** The chain of custody is Dave.
> **KIT:** *(long pause, then typing)* ...I'm going to write "a source."

## Coverage to grab

- The whole rally in one continuous two-shot, plus singles on both — you'll want
  to tighten the rhythm in the edit and singles let you cheat the pacing.
- BEX's "Dave." ×5, all completely unbothered.
- KIT's slow burn at shot 10, one flat and one visibly aging.
