perfectly. It is answering a question nobody asked.

## Script (clean read)

> **ARI:** It verifies.
> **NOOR:** You made that file.
> **ARI:** And it verifies.
> **NOOR:** Yes. That's... that's not the flex you think it is.
> **ARI:** The math says it's intact.
> **NOOR:** The math says you haven't edited your own lie since Tuesday.
> *(pause)*
> **ARI:** ...It's a very consistent lie.
> **NOOR:** It is. That's all it is.

---

## Direction notes

- **This is the thesis skit.** If you only shoot one, shoot this one.
- ARI is genuinely, sincerely triumphant at the top. Not scheming, not smug —
  ARI *believes* the green text settled something. That sincerity is the engine.
- NOOR is not dunking. Shot 7 is delivered as a correction, the way you'd tell
  someone their shoelace is untied. The line is savage enough without help.
- **Shot 8 must be long.** Three seconds minimum of ARI processing. Cut it and
  you lose the best beat in the set.
- Shot 9 is the laugh. Play it tiny.
- Shot 10 lands the actual message. Don't wink at camera.
