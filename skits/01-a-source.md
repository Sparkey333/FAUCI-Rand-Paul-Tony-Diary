# Skit 1 — "A Source"

**Runtime** ~35s · **Format** 9:16 vertical · **Cast** 3 (can be played by 2 with an off-camera voice)

The joke: everyone in the room is certain, nobody has checked anything, and the
one person asking how they know is treated as the problem.

No real person is named or depicted. The characters are invented.

## Script (clean read)

> **MARLA:** I have the diary.
> **DEV:** Run it.
> **PRIYA:** Where'd you get it?
> **MARLA:** A source.
> **PRIYA:** Which source?
> **MARLA:** *(beat)* ...A source's source.
> **DEV:** Run it.
> **PRIYA:** Has anyone checked whether the file's been edited?
> *(silence)*
> **DEV:** ...Run it.

---

## Direction notes

- **Shot 10 is the whole skit.** The laugh is in how long you hold the silence.
  Cut it short and there's no joke. Count three full seconds.
- DEV never looks up. Not once. That's the character.
- MARLA is not stupid — she's *excited*. Play the enthusiasm straight; the
  comedy dies if she plays it knowing.
- PRIYA is not smug. She genuinely wants to know. Sincerity is funnier than
  superiority here.
- Keep it dry throughout. Nobody in this scene thinks they're in a comedy.

## Alternate ending (swap shot 13)

If you'd rather land on the tool than the tag:

> **PRIYA:** *(alone, to camera, flat)* I hashed it. It's a PDF of a screenshot
> of a photo of a page. *(beat)* We won a prize.
