# Alts

Alternate versions of all five skits: 15-second cutdowns for social, swappable
cold opens, alternate punchlines, and a bank of standalone one-liners.

Nothing here needs a reshoot if you grabbed the coverage listed in each script.

---

## 15-second cutdowns

Vertical platforms punish a slow open. Each of these front-loads the hook and
drops everything else.

### 1 — A Source · 14s
> **MARLA:** I have the diary.
> **PRIYA:** Where'd you get it?
> **MARLA:** A source.
> **PRIYA:** Which source?
> **MARLA:** *(beat)* A source's source.
> **DEV:** Run it.

Cut shots 9–12 entirely. The hash question is the *long* version's joke; the
short version's joke is "a source's source." Don't try to keep both.

### 2 — We Never Said That · 15s
> **JO:** They took it down. Now they're saying it never said that.
> **SAM:** Did you seal it?
> *(terminal: `intact: content matches the sealed manifest`)*
> **JO:** So that proves they published it.
> **SAM:** No. It proves your copy hasn't changed since you sealed it.
> **JO:** ...That's significantly less impressive.

Drop the screenshot exchange (shots 2–3). Ends on the deflation, not the moral.

### 3 — The Expert · 13s
> **REESE:** Is it real?
> **VANCE:** The typeface is consistent with the period.
> **REESE:** So it's real.
> **VANCE:** The typeface is consistent with the period.
> **REESE:** Then what am I paying you for?
> **VANCE:** Consistency. With the period.

Two repetitions instead of three. Any more and a 13s cut feels padded.

### 4 — Chain of Custody · 15s
Runs nearly as-is; cut shots 7–9 (the "who else is in it" turn and the
photocopier) and go straight from "Dave's." to "So the chain of custody is Dave."

### 5 — It Verifies · 15s
> **ARI:** It verifies.
> **NOOR:** You made that file.
> **ARI:** And it verifies.
> **NOOR:** The math says you haven't edited your own lie since Tuesday.
> **ARI:** *(small)* ...It's a very consistent lie.

Cut shots 5–6. Keep the pause at 8 — even at 15s, that pause is the skit.

## Alternate cold opens

Swap in place of shot 1 when you want a different energy.

**Cold open A — in medias res** (any skit)
Open mid-argument with an unrelated line, then hard cut to the real first line:
> "—no, because a PDF *can* be a primary source, that's my whole—"
> *(cut)*

**Cold open B — the receipt** (skits 1, 4, 5)
Tight insert of a phone screen: a message reading *"can't say where this came
from but"* — then pull out to the scene.

**Cold open C — direct address** (skits 2, 5)
Character looks straight down the barrel, says one line, never acknowledges the
camera again:
> "Everyone in this room is about to be extremely confident."

**Cold open D — the stack** (skit 3)
Silent. Someone sets down a folder. Then another. Then another. Then another.
Four seconds of folders. Then dialogue.

Collected from all five scripts plus extras. All interchangeable with the tag
card at the end of any skit.

## Tone variants

Each skit has been written **deadpan**, which is the recommended read. Two other
passes are worth shooting if you have the time:

**Chaotic** — everyone talks over everyone. Overlap the dialogue by ~30%, let
lines get stepped on, keep the last line of each skit clean. Works best on
Chain of Custody.

**Corporate** — play it as a meeting. Everyone is pleasant, nobody escalates,
the questions get asked in a tone of gentle process improvement. Devastating on
The Expert and It Verifies; wrong for A Source.

**Do not play any of them as satire of a real named individual.** The characters
are invented and should stay that way — it's a better bit, and it's the version
that can't be misread as a claim about a real person.

## Standalone one-liners

For text posts, thumbnails, or as an on-screen line over a cutaway. Each stands
without the surrounding skit.

- "A source's source."
- "Has anyone checked whether the file's been edited?"
- "Screenshots aren't proof. Anyone can fake a screenshot."
- "The chain of custody is Dave."
- "It's a very consistent lie."
- "'Consistent with the period' is not 'real.'"
- "It verifies. It was always going to verify. You made it."
- "Seal it when you get it, not when you need it."

## Running order

If you post all five, this order builds:

1. **A Source** — sets up the world
2. **Chain of Custody** — fastest, best hook, good second impression
3. **The Expert** — slower, rewards the audience you've kept
4. **We Never Said That** — introduces the tool doing something
5. **It Verifies** — the turn. Land the set here.

Posting 5 first works too, and is the better standalone if you only ship one.
