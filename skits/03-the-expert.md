# Skit 3 — "The Expert"

**Runtime** ~35s · **Format** 9:16 vertical · **Cast** 2

The joke: an expert who is being scrupulously, uselessly correct, and a client
who wants a headline. Nobody is wrong. That's the problem.

No real person is named or depicted. The characters are invented.

## Script (clean read)

> **REESE:** So. Is it real?
> **VANCE:** The typeface is consistent with the period.
> **REESE:** So it's real.
> **VANCE:** The typeface is consistent with the period.
> **REESE:** ...Is it real?
> **VANCE:** I'm not able to say that.
> **REESE:** Then what am I paying you for?
> **VANCE:** Consistency. With the period.

---

## Direction notes

- **VANCE is not being difficult.** They are being *exact*, and they have had
  this conversation two hundred times. No irritation, no smugness — the flatness
  of someone reading a weather report.
- The repeated line must be **identical both times.** Same pitch, same pace, same
  emphasis. If the second reading has any colour in it, the joke becomes VANCE
  being sarcastic, which is a worse joke.
- REESE's escalation is entirely in the pauses, not the volume.
- Give VANCE a physical activity — turning pages, adjusting a lamp — that never
  stops for the conversation.

## Alternate ending (swap shot 10)

> **VANCE:** *(beat)* It's paper.
